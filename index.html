      How the decisions and consensus are made
      

        
Each model rates three criteria (yes, partly or no) and flags any exclusion code. The decision follows from those ratings: 1 if all three are "yes" and nothing is excluded; 0 if an exclusion applies or any criterion is "no"; 2 (uncertain) otherwise, or when the record has too little information.

        
Consensus: a record is marked include or exclude only when both models give the same 0 or 1 and each decision matches that model's own ratings. Everything else is human_review and is listed first in the CSV, with empty human_decision and human_notes columns to fill in. With a single model, its consistent 0 and 1 decisions are used and every 2 goes to review.
